## Getting Started
Setting a reminder in Task 15 is super easy! Here is how the flow works in the app:

1. **Type your task**: On the main screen (after you log in), type the name of the task you want to add in the text box at the top (e.g., "Buy Groceries").
2. **Tap the Bell Icon**: Just below the text box, you will see a purple **bell icon** (with a small plus sign). Tap on it!
3. **Pick the Date**: A calendar will pop up. Choose the date you want the reminder for and click **OK**.
4. **Pick the Time**: Right after you select the date, a clock will pop up. Select the exact time you want your phone to notify you and click **OK**.
5. **Add the Task**: You'll now see your chosen date and time displayed next to the bell icon. Tap the **`+` button** next to the text box to actually save the task!

Once you hit the `+` button, the task is saved to Firebase, and your phone will automatically schedule a background notification to alert you exactly at the time you chose!
